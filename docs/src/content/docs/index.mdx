title: Overview
description: One app for your cameras and recorders, through a small server on your own network.
---

Kestrel is one app for your cameras and recorders: live view, recordings,
events and each one's health, from any browser or as an app on your phone. It
talks to each system directly, so nothing goes through a vendor's cloud and
old hardware stays useful after its own apps stop working.

It works with:

- **Raysharp-made recorders**, which Swann, Lorex, Night Owl and others
  sell, and whose own apps and plugins have stopped working in modern
  browsers.
- **Reolink cameras, hubs and recorders**, over their HTTP API, RTSP and
  Reolink's own protocol.
- **The camera in a Bambu Lab P1 or A1 printer**, as a picture every few
  seconds, with what it records and its timelapses.
- **Any camera with a stream address**, such as RTSP, or a still picture.
- **Live streams on web pages**, such as a Twitch channel or a YouTube live
  stream, through yt-dlp.

The server can also record any of them to your own disk, and copies each
source's own recordings in beside them. See [Running it](/running#your-sources)
for what each kind of source takes.

A small server sits between your cameras and the page. Browsers can't open a
recorder's control port or play its RTSP streams, so the server holds the
sessions, passes on what they report, and runs go2rtc for the video. See
[Running it](/running) to set it up.

## What it does

- **Sources:** each source's connection and, for the recorder, its clock,
  its disks, and each camera's signal, recording mode and motion.
- **Recordings:** which days have recordings, and each day's clips on a
  channel, with why they were recorded (motion, PIR, alarm or normal) and
  their size. The player is laid out as the recorder's own client lays out
  playback: the video, its controls (stop, a frame at a time, speed up to 32
  times, the recordings either side, cut, a still, download, the same zoom
  as Live (fit, fill or a step at a time, only for the visit) and full
  screen, each with a key, listed under the help button), then a timeline of
  the day, where a click plays from that moment, or the nearest recording
  (the nearest event with Events only). It zooms as Chrome DevTools'
  timeline does, down to a minute: a step at a time or a preset (Day, 4 h,
  1 h, 10 min) beside it, Ctrl and the wheel or a pinch at the pointer, and
  while zoomed, an overview of the whole day under it, where a drag across
  zooms to that stretch and a drag on the view moves it. A drag on the
  timeline pans it. Playback runs on from one recording into the
  next, any stretch of a day saves as an MP4, and the page remembers where it
  was, its speed and its timeline zoom in the browser. Up to four cameras play
  together on one clock, ticked in the camera picker: each one that recorded
  at that moment plays, the rest show a placeholder until their next
  recording, and the timeline has a track for each. They play the full
  stream while the recorder keeps up, about 14 times real time across them,
  and the sub stream past that.
- **Activity:** what the server does by itself, such as copying recordings
  from sources, recording a camera and clearing out old recordings, with
  how far through each is, the last 7 days of errors, the recordings copied
  lately and what each camera's recordings take on disk, and the server's
  and the machine's memory and CPU over the last hour, against the limits
  background work keeps to.
- **Settings:** each source and its cameras, their names and order, which
  cameras the server records and how long recordings are kept, the limits
  for background work, the paired devices, and installing the page as an
  app. Its About card shows the page's and the server's versions.
- **Pages:** Live, Recordings, Events, Sources, Activity and Settings each
  have an address of their own, such as `/recordings?camera=swann/1&date=2026-11-11`, or
  `/recordings?camera=swann/1&cameras=swann/1,swann/3,swann/4&date=2026-11-11`
  for several cameras together. Live takes the same: `/?camera=swann/1`
  shows that camera alone, and `/?cameras=swann/1,swann/3` those cameras,
  without changing where Live is left for next time. A camera's id is its
  source's and its own, such as `swann/3` for the recorder's channel 3.
  Older addresses by channel number still work.
- **Embed:** `/embed` is Live on its own, with nothing of the app around it,
  for an iframe on a dashboard or a window of its own. It takes `camera` and
  `cameras` as Live does, and keeps nothing in the browser. Its controls are
  all there until `hide` leaves some out, as a comma list: `controls` (the
  whole bar), `caption` (each camera's name), `layout`, `pause`, `sound`,
  `zoom`, `lens` (a second view in the corner), `picture`, `move`,
  `capture`, `pip` (picture in picture), `help` and `fullscreen`, with their
  keys and gestures. `all`
  leaves out every one, for the plain video, such as
  `/embed?camera=swann/1&hide=all`. Give the iframe `allow="fullscreen"` for
  its full screen button. A browser that isn't paired yet pairs in the
  embed, and goes back to it after.
- **Events:** motion, PIR, video loss and disk events as each source reports
  them, and today's motion and PIR clips found by search.
- **Live view:** the cameras with a signal through go2rtc, all at once (A)
  or any number fewer at a time (from the sub stream past four; 1 to 9 by
  their keys), picked from the Layout menu, with the page beside it: paged
  with Previous and Next (Page Up and Page Down) or picked from the page
  list, which names each camera when there's one at a time. All is the
  default. Smart tiling, the default, sizes the tiles to the window and
  fills as much of it as they can, rows or columns with no empty cells, the
  larger tiles first where the count doesn't split evenly; the Layout menu
  (or T) swaps it for each count's fixed layout, and on a
  phone the fixed layouts list the cameras one under another. The arrow
  keys move the pick around the page and
  on to the next page past the edge. Each picture keeps its shape, fitted
  whole into its tile. The zoom controls act on the picked camera, and each
  camera keeps its own zoom, once in the tiles and once on its own (so one
  zoomed in its tile still shows whole alone). It zooms 25% at a time (+, -
  and 0 to fit it whole), with Fill, where the picture covers its tile, one
  of the steps, so Fit or Fill (Z) jumps between whole and covering. Ctrl
  and the wheel or a pinch zooms the one under the pointer,
  and a drag moves around any picture
  bigger than its tile. Pause closes
  the streams and holds the last frames; a double click or Enter shows the
  picked camera alone, and Esc or Backspace goes back. S saves a still of it,
  I puts it in a picture in picture window, where the browser has one, that
  plays on wherever the app goes and with the tab in the background until
  it's closed (live cameras only, one at a time), and ? shows or hides the
  keys. A camera with a microphone plays its sound
  when it's picked and its sound is on, each with its own volume. One with a
  second view, such as a telephoto lens, plays both, the other small in a
  corner, swapped with L. A camera that moves is steered in Move mode, and
  one with picture settings has its hue, brightness and the rest on sliders
  in the Picture panel. The layout, the tiling, the picked camera, each camera's zoom
  and how many cameras there were are kept in the browser, so the loading
  placeholders already sit where the cameras will.

## Keys and gestures

Live and Recordings list their keys, or their gestures on a touch screen,
under the help button, which ? or a three-finger swipe up opens.

### Live

| Key                   | What it does                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1 to 9, A             | That many cameras at once, or all of them        |
| T                     | Smart tiling, or the Layout menu's fixed layouts |
| Arrow keys            | Pick a camera                                    |
| Page Up, Page Down    | Previous or next cameras                         |
| Enter or double click | The picked camera alone, and back                |
| Esc, Backspace        | Back from one camera, or clear the pick          |
| Space, K              | Pause or play                                    |
| U                     | Sound on or off, where it has a microphone       |
| S                     | Save a still                                     |
| I                     | Picture in picture, which plays on other pages   |
| L                     | Swap its two views, such as wide and telephoto   |
| P                     | Picture settings, where it has them              |
| M                     | Start or end Move mode, for a camera that moves  |
| Arrow keys, in Move   | Turn it while held, or drag the picture          |
| +, -, in Move         | Zoom its lens                                    |

On a touch screen, swipe left or right for the next or previous cameras, up
or down to show or hide the controls in full screen, pinch then drag to
zoom and move around, and double tap for the camera alone.

### Recordings

| Key                   | What it does                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Space, K              | Play or pause                                     |
| [, ]                  | Slower or faster                                  |
| O                     | Events only, skipping what's between              |
| .                     | Next frame                                        |
| Left, Right           | Back or on 5 seconds                              |
| J, L                  | Back or on 10 seconds                             |
| Shift + Left, Right   | Back or on 30 seconds                             |
| W, E                  | Previous or next event, from 20 seconds before it |
| P, N                  | Previous or next recording                        |
| Page Up, Page Down    | Pick the previous or next camera                  |
| Enter or double click | The picked camera alone, and back                 |
| Esc, Backspace        | Back from one camera alone                        |
| T                     | Smart tiling, or the View menu's fixed layouts    |
| C                     | Mark the clip's start, then its end               |
| S                     | Save a still                                      |

On a touch screen, swipe left or right to go on or back 10 seconds, two
fingers left or right for the next or previous recording, up or down to
show or hide the controls in full screen, and pinch then drag to zoom. On
the timeline, drag to move through the day, tap to play from there, and
pinch to show more or less of it.

### Both

| Key           | What it does                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Z             | Fit the picture whole, or fill its tile    |
| +, -, 0       | Zoom in, out, or back to the whole picture |
| Ctrl + scroll | Zoom at the pointer; drag to move around   |
| F             | Full screen                                |
| ?             | The keys and gestures                      |

## Parts

| Part                         | What it is                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `packages/core`              | What every source shares, in TypeScript with Effect                     |
| `packages/protocol-raysharp` | The Raysharp recorders' control protocol                                |
| `packages/source-raysharp`   | Raysharp recorders as a source, over that protocol and their RTSP       |
| `packages/source-stream`     | Cameras you list by their stream addresses, or a still picture          |
| `packages/source-reolink`    | Reolink cameras, hubs and recorders, over their HTTP API and RTSP       |
| `packages/protocol-baichuan` | Reolink's own protocol, for recording and replaying files               |
| `packages/source-bambu`      | The camera in a Bambu Lab P1 or A1 printer, with its recordings         |
| `packages/source-web`        | Live streams from web pages, through yt-dlp                             |
| `packages/protocol-ftp`      | An upload-only FTP receiver, for cameras that send their clips          |
| `packages/format-mp4`        | Recorded video as MP4, written for the browser and read back as frames  |
| `packages/nvr`               | Records streams to disk with ffmpeg, clears out old ones and reads them |
| `packages/server`            | The server: the [API](/api), go2rtc and recording                       |
| `packages/web`               | The web client, installable on a phone                                  |
| `packages/desktop`           | The desktop app, which runs the server and keeps it in the tray         |
| `packages/cli`               | The `kestrel` CLI, with Code Mode and MCP for [agents](/agents)         |
| `docs`                       | This site                                                               |

The [protocol](/protocol) page describes what Kestrel speaks to each kind of
device.

title: Overview
description: A web client and bridge for Raysharp-made recorders, such as the Swann DVR8-4575.

DVR Revival brings an old recorder back into use from a browser. Raysharp makes
recorders for Swann, Lorex, Night Owl and others, and their own apps and
plugins have stopped working in modern browsers. This talks to the recorder
directly instead.

A small server sits between the recorder and the page. Browsers can't open the
recorder's control port or play its RTSP streams, so the server holds one
logged-in session, passes on what it reports, and runs go2rtc for live video.

The plan is tracked in
[timmo001/dvr-revival#1](https://github.com/timmo001/dvr-revival/issues/1). See
[Running it](/running) to set it up.

## What it does

- **Recorder health:** the recorder's clock, its disks, and each channel's
  signal, recording mode and motion.
- **Recordings:** which days have recordings, and each day's clips on a
  channel, with why they were recorded (motion, PIR, alarm or normal) and
  their size. The player is laid out as the recorder's own client lays out
  playback: the video, its controls (stop, a frame at a time, speed up to 32
  times, the recordings either side, cut, a still, download, zoom and full
  screen, each with a key, listed under the help button), then a timeline of
  the day that zooms to ten minutes, where a
  click plays from that moment. Playback runs on from one recording into the
  next, any stretch of a day saves as an MP4, and the page remembers where it
  was, its speed and its zoom in the browser. Up to four channels play
  together on one clock, ticked in the channel picker: each one that recorded
  at that moment plays, the rest show a placeholder until their next
  recording, and the timeline has a track for each. They play the full
  stream while the recorder keeps up, about 14 times real time across them,
  and the sub stream past that.
- **Pages:** Live, Recordings, Events and Recorder each have an address of
  their own, such as `/recordings?channel=1&date=2026-11-11`, or
  `/recordings?channel=1&channels=1,3,4&date=2026-11-11` for several
  channels together.
- **Events:** motion, PIR, video loss and disk reports as the recorder sends
  them, and today's motion and PIR clips found by search.
- **Live view:** the cameras with a signal through go2rtc, 1, 2, 3 or 4 at a
  time (and 6, 9 or 16 on bigger recorders, from the sub stream), paged with
  Previous and Next. Pause closes the streams and holds the last frames; a
  double click or Enter shows the picked camera alone, and S saves a still
  of it. The layout and the picked camera are kept in the browser.

## Parts

| Part              | What it is                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `packages/rsnet`  | The recorder's control protocol, in TypeScript with Effect          |
| `packages/server` | The server: the recorder session, go2rtc and the [API](/api)        |
| `packages/web`    | The web client, installable on a phone                              |
| `packages/cli`    | The `dvr-revival` CLI, with Code Mode and MCP for [agents](/agents) |
| `docs`            | This site                                                           |

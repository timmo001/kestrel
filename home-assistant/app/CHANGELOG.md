Live cameras can now pop out into a picture in picture window that keeps playing wherever you go.

## Live

- A new Picture in picture button on the Live controls, or the I key, puts the picked camera (or the first shown) in the browser's floating window. It keeps playing on every other page, while Live is paused, as the layout changes and with the tab in the background, until you close the window. Press it again to close it.
- The camera's tile keeps playing on the page, holding its last frame for the moment it takes to start again. Its sound moves to the window, at the camera's own volume, and follows the volume control.
- Paused, the window starts its own stream from the camera's main stream.
- One camera at a time: picking another moves the window to it. Recordings don't go there, only live cameras.
- The button shows only where the browser has picture in picture.

## Embed

- `hide` takes `pip` to leave the button and its key out of an embed.

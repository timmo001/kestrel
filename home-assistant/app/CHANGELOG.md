The Recordings timeline works like a camera app's now, both players share one set of controls, and things move rather than jump.

## Recordings

- The timeline's playhead is a line fixed in the middle, and the day moves under it. Drag the day to scrub: the time shows over the line, the video follows where that part has loaded, and it plays from where you let go. A quick flick carries on a little first.
- A click or tap on the timeline still jumps there, snapped to the nearest recording, or event with Events only on, and the day slides so that spot sits under the line.
- ‹ and › buttons step along the day when it's zoomed in, and only show while there's more that way. A step or drag that stops in a gap goes on to the next recording the way you were moving.
- Pinch, or Ctrl and the wheel, zooms the timeline. The small overview strip under it is gone.
- The player, its controls and the timeline are one card. Cameras are picked from a menu at the start of the card, and Events only is a toggle, folding into the zoom menu when space is tight.
- The Recordings help is grouped by what each key does, and lists the touch gestures, the timeline's included.

## Live

- Picture settings open as a popover by their button on a computer, and as a sheet from the bottom on a phone. The moving setup keeps its sheet.
- The layout picker fits as many layouts as there's room for, with the rest in a More menu.
- Camera names shrink on small tiles, and hide with the full screen controls.
- Preset and home presses show as pending until the camera answers, and you can still press again. Capture, picture changes and reset do the same.
- The Live help groups its keys by what they act on, with shorter descriptions.

## Both players

- Live and Recordings share one control bar. On a phone on its side, or full screen, it sits over the bottom of the picture, see-through, showing its first row until opened and fading while playing. Pull it down to hide it; a short pull springs back.
- Single-key shortcuts only act while the player has focus, so typing elsewhere on the page doesn't trigger them.
- Controls stay clear of rounded screen corners and the middle of the side edges on a phone on its side.
- While one of the controls' menus or popovers is open, they stay up, and a tap outside only closes it.

## Motion

- Pages slide in the way you're going, the Move panel slides up from the bottom (from the side on a phone on its side), and tiles travel to their new places when the layout changes. With reduced motion on, they crossfade instead.

## Fixes

- Pills over the picture are see-through, and a tile no longer flashes a ring when it arrives.

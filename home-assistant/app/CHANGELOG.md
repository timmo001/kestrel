An embed showing one camera now gives it the whole window, so a picture that isn't 16:9, such as a doorbell's 4:3, fills it rather than sitting small in a box.

## Embed

- With one camera, `/embed` fits its picture whole to the full frame or window. Before, it was boxed into a 16:9 tile inside the window, so a 4:3 picture had black bands on every side, and the controls covered its bottom edge.
- A narrow embed with one camera no longer drops into the phone's stacked layout when Live was last left on fixed layouts.

## Fixes

- Nothing else changes for Live or Recordings: several cameras in an embed, and every camera on Live, still tile as before.

Pairing a phone or another browser now uses a short code you can copy or a QR code, and full screen on a phone works properly again: its sheets and the help scroll, and the volume no longer asks to discard changes.

## Devices

- Pairing a device opens a small panel with a code you can copy, a link that pairs straight away, and a QR code that's always shown.

## Phones and touch screens

- Table headings, fields and small buttons side by side take a full finger-sized press on a touch screen, without reaching into their neighbours.
- A recording's Play and Download buttons no longer overlap when pressed with a finger.
- On a narrow screen the zoom controls on the player bars fit their row rather than spilling past it.
- In Settings, each source's Remove button shows only its icon on a phone, so the source's name stays on one line.

## Fixes

- In full screen, the moving camera setup sheet and the picture settings can be pressed and scrolled again. Before, they showed but ignored every touch.
- The help opened while the controls had hidden themselves, such as with a three finger swipe up, now scrolls and takes presses rather than passing them to the picture.
- Changing the volume no longer counts as an unsaved edit, so closing it never asks "Discard your changes?".
